Converse One Star Pro Suede

Men's Casual Shoes

THE ONE YOU NEED In 1974, Converse launched a low-cut suede version of the Chuck Taylor All Star, featuring a lone star logo on one side—the One Star sneaker. Re-released in the early ’90s, the design gained traction among the skateboarding community thanks to its performance overhaul. Today’s One Star Pro helps deliver improved board feel and shock protection with CX Foam cushioning. The simple design adds flexibility, while the full suede upper keeps it durable and timeless.
