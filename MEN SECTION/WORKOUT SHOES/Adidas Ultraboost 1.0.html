Adidas Ultraboost 1.0

ENERGISED SHOES MADE IN PART WITH PARLEY OCEAN PLASTIC.

Once laced up, the Ultraboost 1.0 shoes unleash energy and comfort with every stride. Primeknit wraps your foot in a supportive second skin that flexes and moves as you do. A Boost midsole returns the energy you put in, keeping your step light and responsive from commute to cafe. The casual yet performance-ready design carries the DNA of our popular running shoe into the rhythms of your day. Wherever the city takes you, Ultraboost comes along for the ride. This product is made with a yarn that features 50% recycled polyester and 50% Parley Ocean Plastic — plastic waste which we intercepted from remote islands, beaches, coastal communities and shorelines in order to prevent it from polluting our oceans. In total, this product has 20% recycled content.
